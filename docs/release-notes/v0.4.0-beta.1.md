# Stedding 0.4.0 beta 1

Chromium 157, the address field at the width you like, and pinned tabs that sleep before they
go. On Chromium 157.0.8103.31.

## New

- **Chromium 157**, the next stable milestone, which Google rolls out to everyone from
  3 November.
- **The address field, your width.** Settings › Stedding › Address field: Full width, as before,
  or a field centred on the page, Wide, Medium or Compact. A centred field stays put while you
  type, the suggestions open under it, and the empty row beside it moves the window (#14).
- **Pinned tabs sleep first.** While a pinned tab is awake, on screen or not, its button is a
  **−** that puts it to sleep, keeping it pinned. Once it is asleep the button is an **x** that
  asks *Remove this pinned tab?* first (#50).
- **Open pinned tabs stay in view.** A collapsed folder keeps showing the pinned tabs in it that
  are awake, so going back to one does not take opening the folder (#35).

## Fixed

- Switching to a Space shows the tab you used there last, an essential included. It had shown
  the Space's first pinned tab (#42).
- A tab dropped onto a pinned folder, or opened with **New Tab in Folder**, becomes one of its
  pinned tabs. It had joined unpinned, and the folder then left the pinned tabs.
- With Google as the search engine, the focused address field no longer offers *Ask Google about
  this page* (#14).

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.1-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.1-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone.
  It is not signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- Chromium's built-in list of sites that must load over HTTPS lapses for this build on
  13 December 2026; a release on a newer base comes before then.

## How it was checked

Every unit and browser test on Windows after the last change (1290 unit tests; 728 browser
tests, 158 of them one at a time), and the Mac's 1294 unit tests: all passed. The Mac's 763
browser tests (152 of them one at a time) passed before the fix for Remove, and the sidebar
click tests, which hold its test, after it. Each change has a test. On the Mac's signed build,
the acceptance scripts (the core loop, a relaunch, drags, keys) passed, their captures the same
as 0.3.0 beta 22's apart from what changed; with the real pointer, a pinned tab's Remove closed
it without a crash, with the animations on and off. Apple's distribution check passed the
signed app. On Windows the 39 acceptance checks passed. On both, an installed 0.3.0 beta 22
updated itself to this release through a copy of the update feed. Builds with extra checks
and with AddressSanitizer were still running at release; what they find goes into beta 2.
