# Feature: History and Downloads in the sidebar

Status: **Pn1–Pn16 built** (written 2026-10-25, BACKLOG S-98), released in 0.3.0 beta 16, Pn15
as it is now in beta 17; Pn1–Pn14 and Pn16 pass their tests on the Mac (2026-10-26). The
operator asked in chat on 2026-10-25 for the next big features to be built, which lifts `PLAN.md`'s "defects before features" gate for this one. **On by default**: Settings ›
Stedding › Sidebar › "Open History and Downloads in the sidebar" turns it off.

History and Downloads open as panels in the sidebar's own column, in the place of the
essentials and the tab list, between the sidebar's top row and its foot. The foot stays: the
archive line with the Archived row and the History button, the update pill, and the bottom row
with the downloads button, the Space switcher and its "+" (sidebar Y44). ⌘Y and Ctrl+H open History; ⇧⌘J or ⌥⌘L and Ctrl+J open
Downloads; the same key again closes the panel.

The panel is a web view of `chrome://stedding-library/history` or `/downloads`, a page built
into the browser. It reuses Chromium's own History and Downloads handlers for the data
(`BrowsingHistoryHandler`, `DownloadsDOMHandler`) and adds the verbs they lack through its own
(`stedding::LibraryHandler`: open a page in the window, Copy File, Move to Trash, close, Open
as Page). Which panel a window shows is the window's `stedding::SidebarPanels`
(`chrome/browser/ui/stedding/sidebar_panels.h`), owned by its sidebar and found through the
window's user data, so the window's commands -- the keys, the menus, the command bar's Show
History and Show Downloads rows -- open a panel without the views. The view is
`stedding::SidebarPanelView`; the sidebar makes it when a panel opens and drops it, after the
call that closed it has returned, when the panel closes.

Chromium's own side panel is not used: at this pin its History entry is behind a flag that is
off, it has no Downloads entry, and it is a column at the window's right, not the sidebar.

## While a panel is open

- The tab list is under the panel, not gone: a Space switch, ⌘T, a new tab or a tab closed by
  key changes it as always, and it shows again when the panel closes. Rows cannot be dragged
  while it is hidden.
- The panel's page keeps its search and its scroll only while it is open: a switch to the
  other panel, or a close, drops it. No panel is open after a restart.
- The keys and menus that are not History's or Downloads' still work, the focus in the panel
  or not (Pn1).

## What still opens the pages

The panels replace the keys, the menus and the command bar's rows, which run the window's
History and Downloads commands. Some paths open the pages themselves and are kept, since each
is the way to the full page: the History page's own links, the downloads bubble's "See all
downloads", a click on the downloads button when the bubble is not available, the action
behind the toolbar's pinned History button, and Open as Page.

## Privacy

Nothing is fetched (Pn8). The History panel asks Chromium's own history handler, which can read
web history only for a profile signed in to Google with history sync on; Stedding offers no
sign-in (`docs/PRIVACY.md`, "Google account sync -- not available"), so it reads the local
history alone. Copy File writes a file's path to the clipboard only when the user chooses it
(Pn10).
