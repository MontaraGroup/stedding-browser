# Feature: Pinned tabs

Status: **H1–H6, H8–H18 built** (H15–H17 since beta 14; H18, and the changes of 2026-10-18 to H1, H3, H9, H14 and H16, not yet tried on a real build); **H7 partial** (only Peek reads it, and Peek is off until rebuilt, BACKLOG S-93).
Owner docs: `docs/PRODUCT.md` §1, `docs/features/spaces.md` (the two tiers). Patches: 0020, 0024, 0037 (H12), 0057 (H13), 0059 (H14), 0060 (H15, H16), 0061 (H17), and the defect round of 2026-10-18 (H18 and the changes above).

Arc's pinned-tab lifecycle: a pin remembers its home page, ⌘W puts it to sleep
instead of closing it, a drifted pin shows a dot and resets from its favicon.
