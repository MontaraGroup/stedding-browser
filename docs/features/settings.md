Stedding's own settings live in one section of chrome://settings, listed first and
carrying the Stedding mark. Every row is one preference — Stedding's own in `chrome/browser/ui/stedding/stedding_prefs.h`
(with the default its feature spec names), or a Chromium preference for a behaviour Stedding leans on — read by
exactly one feature. Most are the profile's; the sidebar's density and text size, Global
Privacy Control and the Updates rows are the browser's own (local state), shared by every
profile. A feature that
gains a preference adds a row here and a behaviour in its own spec.
