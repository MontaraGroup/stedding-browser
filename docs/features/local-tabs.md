# Feature: Local tabs

Status: **Lt1, Lt2, Lt7, Lt10 and Lt14 built, the rest partial**: written 2026-10-18; on 2026-10-19 the browser suites `LocalTabsTest` and `LocalTabsHorizontalTest` (18 tests) and the unit suites `LocalPagesTest`, `TabRowRulesTest` and `LocalRowLinesTest` passed on the Mac (not yet run on Windows), and nothing has been tried on a real build; the captures (Lt4, Lt9, Lt13) and VoiceOver and Narrator (Lt12) not yet done, the live checks in `docs/features/acceptance.md`, "Local tabs". BACKLOG S-117.
Owner docs: `docs/PRODUCT.md` §12 (Developer Mode), `docs/features/toolbar.md` T11, `docs/features/settings.md` T19. Patch: none yet (proposed: the next in the series).

A tab showing a page from this machine's own web server looks different in the sidebar,
as in Arc's Developer Mode. The row of the local tab on screen wears Arc's yellow and
black tape round its edge, and at the Comfortable density every local row shows its host
and port in small text under its title, so a developer tells `localhost:3000` from
`localhost:5173` at a glance. "Local" is the rule the address row already uses (toolbar
T11): `http` or `https` on `localhost`, a name under `.localhost`, `127.0.0.0/8` or
`[::1]`. Arc draws the outline "when focused", turns it on for any localhost site and has
no global setting, only a per-site switch Stedding does not have yet. Stedding adds one
setting, on by default.

No chord and no Mac-only part. The two platforms draw the sidebar in different fonts (the
system font on the Mac, Segoe UI on Windows), so where a row shows two lines is decided by
the density (Lt6), not by measuring the fonts: the outcome is the same on both.

## Notes

- Arc's own documentation (Arc Help Center, "Developer Mode: Instant Dev Tools"): "Developer
  Mode tabs have a yellow and black outline when focused", on for any localhost site and its
  subdomains, per site rather than global, macOS only. What Arc draws on a local row that is
  not focused is not documented; the host line on every Comfortable local row is Stedding's
  choice, so a local row in the background is still told apart there. At Compact and Dense
  it is not (Lt6); whether those densities should get a mark of their own is the owner's
  call.
- The colours are fixed, not theme colours, so a custom theme cannot turn the tape into
  something that does not read as hazard tape. The yellow is a reading of Arc's from one
  screenshot; the stroke and dash sizes are too, and may be Retina pixels (then half).
- The two lines are measured by their text, not by their labels' line boxes: a label's line
  is at least the typography's 20 DIP, and two of those never fit a 44 DIP row with room to
  spare. The title keeps its box and moves up; the host line's box is its text's height.
- Contrast (Lt4, Lt5) is measured against the bare ground and the active row's fill with the
  window active. Hovered and selected fills and any Space gradient over the frame colour are
  not measured. In a window that is not the active one every row's text is dimmer, by
  Chromium's rule, and the host line with it: 85% of a title made for 4.5:1 then falls below
  4.5:1.
- Arc's other Developer Mode parts are not here: the per-site switch and Ctrl-D, the toolbar
  with Inspect, Console, Network and Portrait Mode, and the JSON Formatter it installs on its
  own. The address row (toolbar T11) is built. The rest stays with BACKLOG S-117; a silent
  extension install is not something Stedding does.
- Browser tests serve local pages from `embedded_test_server()->GetURL("localhost", ...)` and
  other sites from `GetURL("a.test", ...)` with `host_resolver()->AddRule("*", "127.0.0.1")`.
  A bare `GetURL("/x")` is `127.0.0.1`, which is local: the active rows of most existing
  sidebar tests wear the tape now, and their Comfortable rows show a host line, which is why
  Lt6 keeps every height and the title's column. The tabs, density, sidebar, pins and
  addressrow suites are still to be run before and after this change.
