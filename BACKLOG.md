# Backlog

**This file is the order of work.** [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) holds the owner's standing
decisions and the rules for when an item is done. Ids from the old plan (TAB-1, SPC-4,
...) are cited as they are and resolve in
[docs/history/plan-2026-09-26.md](docs/history/plan-2026-09-26.md).

The one list. Other documents cite ids from here; they do not keep lists of their own.
Ids are stable: never renumber, never reuse. Close an item by moving its row, with the commit
or patch that closed it, to the top of "Closed backlog items" in
[docs/history/archive.md](docs/history/archive.md); `tooling/check-repo` reads ids from both.

Open is in priority order: the owner's next item, then defects, then checks that are owed,
then infrastructure, then features. The operator's own request in chat comes before this
list (the address bar on 2026-09-30, the hidden sidebar on 2026-10-10, and patches 0060
and 0061 on 2026-10-11 were built at once on their word). Feature behaviour ids (`B<n>`)
refer to `docs/features/<feature>.md`.
